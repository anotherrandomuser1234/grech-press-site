About Grech Press

Grech Press is the publishing imprint of Mark Grech, based in Switzerland. We publish short, practical digital guides — the current typeset editions run 99 to 127 pages — on money, mind, health, home skills, small-business AI and career change.

What we publish

Each guide is built around a small number of useful ideas, with a source list, honest limits and no claims we cannot support. Covers and graphics are programmatically generated SVGs; the text is drafted with AI assistance (AI-assisted) and reviewed and edited by a human before release.

Products are delivered as PDF (and EPUB where listed) via Gumroad. Prices are in USD. No subscription, no upsell funnel. As of 2026-11-24, five paid titles are live, six are drafts and the rest are in preparation.

Editorial process

  • A cited source list per book — 5 to 12 sources in the current v2 editions.
  • Reading-grade and clarity checks, plus a human editorial pass.
  • Explicit limits: no medical, legal, tax or financial advice; no guaranteed outcomes.
  • Facts that change with policy or evidence are framed as “check current guidance”.
